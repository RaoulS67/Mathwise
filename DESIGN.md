# Design System: MathWise

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Day Path Studio"**

MathWise’s interface is a calm, light studio for North Shore parents choosing maths support — trustworthy, unrushed, and clear. A cool off-white canvas holds white panels; Harbour Signal blue is the guiding accent, not wallpaper. The home experience is a path: a quiet vertical line connects hero → team → about → CTA.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light cool canvas (`#F4F7FB`) with soft Signal washes and sparse constellation
- Harbour Signal (`#2F7FC4`) as the sole primary accent — no indigo/lilac partners
- Newsreader display + IBM Plex Sans UI
- Solid Signal CTAs (no blue→indigo gradients); 14px button radius
- Signature path as the home narrative device

## Colors

### Primary
- **Harbour Signal** (`#2F7FC4`): Links, prices, path, focus, primary CTAs
- **Harbour Bright** (`#4B9FE8`): Hover / pressed

### Neutral
- **Canvas** (`#F4F7FB`): Page background
- **Panel** (`#FFFFFF`): Cards, form, team shell
- **Panel muted** (`#E8EEF6`): Fields, nested surfaces
- **Border** (`#D5DEEA`): Default hairlines
- **Ink** (`#1A2332`): Primary text
- **Sub** (`#5A6B7D`): Supporting copy, inactive nav

### Functional
- **Success green** (`#2F9E6E`): Checklist ticks only
- **Star gold** (`#C9A227`): Review stars and gift icons only

**The Signal Rarity Rule.** Harbour Signal stays a minority — accents, links, path, CTAs — never full-bleed wallpaper.

**The No-Indigo Rule.** Never use Tailwind blue-500/600 (`#3b82f6`, `#2563eb`), indigo, lilac, or violet as gradient partners or path stops.

**The Solid CTA Rule.** Primary buttons are solid Signal + white label. No `from-blue to-indigo` fills.

## Typography

**Display:** Newsreader (hero H1, page titles, card titles)  
**Body/UI:** IBM Plex Sans (nav, body, forms)

## Layout

Unchanged path spine and staggered home flow. Pricing remains three product tiers; High School may use a featured border for emphasis.

## Elevation

Soft cool shadows only (`0 8px 24px rgba(26,35,50,0.08)`). No neon zero-offset blue bloom. No glass `backdrop-filter` on review cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 14px radius (`--mw-radius-btn`); not full pills
- **Primary:** solid Harbour Signal fill, white label, modest cool shadow
- **Hover:** Harbour Bright fill, slight lift

### Cards
- White panel, cool border, soft shadow; High School pricing card uses 2px Signal border

### Inputs
- Muted panel field fill, 8px radius, Signal focus ring

### Navigation
- Sub ink links; active/hover Signal; Ink→Signal logo wordmark

## Shapes

Primary CTAs use **14px** radius. Offer badges may stay pill. No floating eyebrow chips above closing CTAs. No colored left-border strips on cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Keep Day Path light canvas + Harbour Signal + path narrative
- Use Newsreader + IBM Plex Sans
- Prefer solid CTAs and quiet shadows

### Don't
- Don't ship permanent dark neon defaults or purple orbs
- Don't introduce cream-education tropes or Inter/Poppins/Geist stacks
- Don't pair Signal with indigo/lilac stops
- Don't invent additional testimonials beyond the confirmed ones on Home
