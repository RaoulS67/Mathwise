# Design System: MathWise

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Path Studio"**

MathWise’s interface is a calm, dark studio for parents choosing maths support — trustworthy, unrushed, and clear. Near-black canvases with a soft night atmosphere hold soft, lifted panels; Signal Blue is the guiding accent, not wallpaper. The home experience is literally a path: a glowing vertical line connects hero → team → about → CTA, so the page feels like a quiet journey rather than a brochure stack.

Density stays measured. Large flow gaps and generous card padding slow the scroll; type is system UI for familiarity, with heavy display weights reserved for parent-facing headlines. Motion is purposeful (path draw, gentle card fade-ins, soft hover lift) — never frantic.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black canvas with soft night atmosphere (low Signal Blue radials + Night Slate depth), sparse constellation dots in side gutters, and ink panels; Signal Blue is the sole primary accent
- Soft pill CTAs and eyebrow chips; inviting, not corporate-sharp
- Lifted glow depth on hero shells and key cards
- Signature animated path as the home-page narrative device
- Logo wordmark uses a white→Signal Blue gradient; preserve logo assets

## Colors

A dark, cool palette: almost no chroma except Signal Blue (plus mint checks and amber stars as functional accents).

### Primary
- **Signal Blue** (`#5ea0ff`): Links, active nav, prices, emphasis (`.mw-em`), social/contact icons, path stroke, offer badges. Used sparingly so it reads as guidance.
- **Signal Blue Deep** (`#3a7bff`): Gradient partner on primary CTAs and soft accent fills.

### Neutral
- **Void Black** (`#000000`): Solid page-canvas fallback under the soft night atmosphere layers.
- **Canvas wash** (`rgba(94, 160, 255, 0.14)` / `0.09`): Sitewide body radials (top-left primary, opposite secondary) — atmosphere only, not accent wallpaper.
- **Panel Ink** (`#0f131a`): Cards, team shell, pricing cards, privacy panel.
- **Panel Soft** (`#0b0f19`): Softer nested surfaces / field backgrounds in the dark system.
- **Night Slate** (`#020617`): Deep radial stops (including bottom canvas depth), gradient button text, pill underlays.
- **Border Ink** (`#1c2230`): Default panel borders.
- **Mist Text** (`#e8edf1`): Primary body/headline text.
- **Cool Sub** (`#aab6c7`): Supporting copy, inactive nav, footer.

### Functional accents
- **Success Mint** (`#4ade80`): Checklist and feature ticks only.
- **Star Amber** (`#ffd166` / `#facc15`): Review stars and gift/offer icons only.

**The Signal Rarity Rule.** Signal Blue should remain a minority of any screen — accents, links, path, and CTAs — never full-bleed backgrounds or large text blocks.

**The Dark Continuity Rule.** All marketing surfaces (including Enrol) belong on the void/panel system — Panel Ink form card, Panel Soft fields, Signal Blue focus and gradient submit.

## Typography

**Display Font:** system-ui stack (system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)  
**Body Font:** same stack  
**Label/Mono Font:** same stack (uppercase labels via tracking, not a separate face)

**Character:** Familiar OS UI type kept calm and parent-readable; hierarchy comes from weight and size, not ornamental fonts.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, 2.85rem, 1.05): Home hero title only.
- **Headline** (800, ~2–2.5rem, ~1.15): Pricing page title, large section headers.
- **Title** (700–800, 1.5–1.8rem): Team title, card titles, pricing tier names, privacy H1.
- **Body** (400–600, 0.92–1rem, 1.6–1.7): Supporting copy; keep ~60ch comfort where max-width is set (~520–600px).
- **Label** (600, ~0.76rem, 0.13em tracking, uppercase): Eyebrow pills (MATHWISE TUTORING, READY TO GET STARTED?).

**The Weight Ladder Rule.** Reserve 900 for hero display; section titles stay 700–800; body never competes with Signal Blue emphasis spans.

## Layout

Single-column marketing shell inside `.container` with horizontal padding `24px`. Max widths: home `1400px`, pricing/enrol `1200px`, privacy `1000px`.

Home uses a staggered flow: content blocks at `min(58%, 720px)` alternating/centering with large vertical rhythm (`gap: 400px` desktop, `220px` ≤900px, `160px` ≤600px) so the path has room to meander. Review rows are 2-column grids (`max-width: 1200px`, `gap: 72px`). Pricing is a centered flex wrap of fixed-width cards (~320px). Navbar stacks at `900px`; footer stacks at `700px`.

Spacing rhythm from tokens: 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px for component padding; section margins ~48px; home narrative uses the oversized `400px` flow gap intentionally.

**The Path Spine Rule.** On home, major sections should remain path-anchorable (origin → anchors → end). Do not collapse the long vertical rhythm into a tight card stack without a replacement narrative device.

## Elevation & Depth

**Lifted glow.** Depth comes from heavy dark umbras plus blue ambient bloom on hero/CTA shells, not from flat Material elevation alone.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hero shell** (`0 24px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.95), 0 0 40px rgba(46,144,255,0.35)`): Primary marketing stage.
- **Pricing CTA shell** (`0 18px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`): Closing conversion stage.
- **Review card** (`0 16px 40px rgba(12, 30, 66, 0.45)` + light backdrop blur): Soft glass-like lift.
- **Pricing card hover** (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` + `translateY(-6px)`): Invitation, not drama.
- **Primary CTA glow** (`0 12px 24px -8px rgba(59,130,246,0.4)` or stronger gradient-button bloom): Action emphasis.
- **Path stroke** (`drop-shadow(0 0 6px rgba(94,160,255,0.6))`): Signature line energy.

Surfaces also use radial gradients (top-left Signal wash into Night Slate / Void) and 1px cool borders (`rgba(37,99,235,…)` or Border Ink).

**The Resting Quiet Rule.** At rest, most panels stay modest; reserve the double-shadow + blue bloom for hero and final CTA shells.

## Shapes

Soft geometry throughout: large continuous radii on shells (`26–30px`), cards at `18–24px`, media at `12–24px`, and **pills everywhere** for CTAs, chips, offer badges, social icons, and contact capsules (`999px` / `40–60px`).

Borders are thin (1px), cool, and often Signal-tinted on interactive or featured surfaces. Images clip to rounded frames; no hard rectangles on primary marketing cards.

**The Soft Invite Rule.** Prefer pill CTAs and 16–24px card radii over sharp rectangles or hairline “editorial” corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Full pill (`999px`)
- **Primary (hero CTA):** Solid Signal-adjacent blue (`#3b82f6`) on white text; hover darkens to `#2563eb` and scales `1.02`
- **Gradient primary (pricing / closing CTA):** `linear-gradient(120deg, #5ea0ff, #3a7bff)` on Night Slate text, heavy blue bloom; hover `translateY(-2px) scale(1.03)`
- **Feel:** Soft and inviting — generous padding (`14px 28px`), weight 600–800

### Chips
- **Eyebrow pills:** Uppercase, wide tracking, Signal-tinted border, radial blue wash on Night Slate
- **Offer badge:** Soft Signal fill (`rgba(94,160,255,0.15)`), pill radius, amber gift icon
- **About chip:** Smaller uppercase pill on dark slate with Border Ink edge

### Cards / Containers
- **Panel card:** Panel Ink, Border Ink, `18px` radius, modest padding — about, team shell, privacy
- **Pricing card:** `24px` radius, centered, hover lift + Signal border
- **Review card:** Gradient Signal wash, `18px`, blur, quote icon + amber stars
- **Hero / pricing shells:** Double-radius frame (outer ~26–30px, inner ~24–26px) with radial glow border

### Inputs / Fields
- **Enrol / dark fields:** Panel Soft inputs on Panel Ink form card; Mist Text; `8px` radius; Signal Blue focus ring; gradient pill submit matching pricing CTAs

### Navigation
- Logo mark (`images/logo1.png`, 40px) + gradient wordmark; favicon is `images/favicon.png` (compressed)
- Links Cool Sub → Signal Blue on hover/active; min touch height `44px` with `10px 14px` padding; stacks centered under `900px`
- Bottom border uses translucent Signal line (`rgba(94,160,255,0.3)`)

### Signature: Decorative Path
- Full-height SVG stroke through `.mw-deco-wrap`, Signal gradient stroke + glow segment
- Anchored from hero origin through section anchors to pricing path-end
- Keep as the home signature; do not reduce to a static divider

### Icons
- Local mask icons in `css/icons.css` (no Font Awesome CDN) — arrow, calendar, quote, star, location, calculator, gift, check, phone, envelope

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Void Black + soft night atmosphere + Panel Ink + Signal Blue as the default surface system.
- **Do** use pill CTAs with soft hover lift/scale for conversion actions.
- **Do** preserve logo assets and the white→Signal Blue gradient wordmark treatment.
- **Do** keep checklist ticks Success Mint and review stars Star Amber — not Signal Blue.
- **Do** keep Enrol (and any new forms) on the dark panel language.

### Don't:
- **Don't** flood screens with Signal Blue backgrounds; rarity is the accent’s job.
- **Don't** introduce a second primary accent family (purple, teal branding, warm cream education tropes).
- **Don't** reintroduce a light Calibri enrol card — stay on Panel Ink.
- **Don't** replace the home path narrative with a dense dashboard of cards.
- **Don't** invent testimonials, school logos, or result claims as visual decoration.
