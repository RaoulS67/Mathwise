# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are North Shore parents deciding whether to book a free diagnostic trial for their child (Years 3–12). The site’s job is to convert that visit into a sign-up / trial booking.

Students are a secondary audience. MathWise intends to reach them mainly through social media so they encourage parents toward this site, not as the primary decision-makers on the site itself.

## Product Purpose

MathWise is a maths tutoring service for Years 3–12, delivered online or in person across Sydney’s North Shore. It exists to move students from partial understanding to independent problem-solving, with targeted support for classwork, school exams, and the HSC.

Success for the website is trial bookings and enrolments via the Enrol Now form (and direct contact).

## Positioning

A small, specialised tutoring team whose tutors have recently completed the HSC themselves, combined with flexible online or in-person delivery on the North Shore. Curriculum alignment to NESA outcomes and current exam practice is part of that claim. Larger centres cannot truthfully copy the “recent high-achieving HSC tutors + local flexibility” pairing.

## Operating Context

- Static marketing site: Home, Pricing, Enrol Now, Privacy Policy.
- Enrolment: Formspree form on Enrol Now (name, email, message about the child); payments are arranged separately, not on the site.
- Delivery: 60-minute sessions; online or in-person across the North Shore (Chatswood to Hornsby, including St Ives and listed suburbs on the site).
- Teaching ritual stated on the site: Understand → Practise → Reinforce → Check.
- Social presence used for student-facing reach: Instagram and Facebook (TikTok link present but not confirmed active).
